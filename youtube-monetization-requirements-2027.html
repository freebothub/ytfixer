YouTube Monetization Requirements 2027: 8,000 Hours / 20M Shorts From 1 Feb 2027

YTFixer Team — 8 November 2026 · Monetization guide

If you plan to apply for YouTube monetization, put one date in your calendar first: 1 February 2027. From that date, new applicants to the YouTube Partner Program, known as YPP, need double the watch time or double the Shorts views they need today. The subscriber requirement stays the same, but the viewing bar rises sharply, and a rushed application just before the deadline can leave you worse off than a careful application after it.

What is the YouTube Partner Program?

The YouTube Partner Program is the route through which a channel becomes eligible to earn money from YouTube. You must meet the eligibility requirements, apply through YouTube Studio, complete the AdSense step, and pass a review of your whole channel, not just your most popular video. A channel can meet every number and still be rejected if the content does not pass that review. Meeting the requirements does not guarantee income or approval, and it does not protect a channel that breaks the rules after it is accepted.

YouTube monetization requirements right now

Right now, an applicant needs 1,000 subscribers, plus one of the two viewing routes below. You do not need both routes. You need one of them, alongside the subscriber count.

RouteWhat you need nowFrom 1 February 2027
Long-form watch time1,000 subscribers + 4,000 valid public watch hours in 12 months1,000 subscribers + 8,000 valid public watch hours in 12 months
Shorts views1,000 subscribers + 10 million valid public Shorts views in 90 days1,000 subscribers + 20 million valid public Shorts views in 90 days

Three words in that table do a lot of work. The first is valid: only valid public watch time and valid public Shorts views count. The second is public: private and unlisted videos do not help you reach the line. The third is the time window: 12 months for watch hours, and 90 days for Shorts views. Check the current figures inside YouTube Studio on the day you apply.

What changes on 1 February 2027

From 1 February 2027, new applicants need 8,000 watch hours instead of 4,000, or 20 million Shorts views instead of 10 million. The subscriber requirement remains 1,000. Channels already in the YouTube Partner Program are not affected: they do not have to re-qualify at the new, higher level simply because the date passed. If you are not yet in the program, it is the deadline your planning should work around.

Who should try to apply before 1 February 2027?

Only creators who can apply cleanly before the date. A clean application means your numbers are genuinely met, your AdSense setup is ready, and your content would pass a review today. If your content still has problems, rushing to beat the date is a trap: a rejection in late January puts your reapplication 30 days later, after the thresholds have risen. If a reviewer looked at your whole channel today, would it pass? If yes, work towards an application before the change. If no, fix the channel first.

The long-form route and the Shorts route

The long-form route needs 1,000 subscribers and 4,000 valid public watch hours in the last 12 months now, or 8,000 hours from 1 February 2027. Watch hours are not the same as views: one viewer watching a 20-minute video to the end gives you far more watch time than twenty viewers who leave after ten seconds. Check retention in YouTube Studio and fix the moment where viewers leave, allowing for older hours that will fall out of the window.

The Shorts route needs 1,000 subscribers and 10 million valid public Shorts views in the last 90 days now, or 20 million from 1 February 2027. Shorts views accumulate fast, but the 90-day window makes this route volatile, so consistency matters more than a single viral Short. A Shorts channel also faces the same content review as a long-form channel, and Shorts that are compilations, re-uploads, or mass-produced templated videos can be rejected for reused content or inauthentic content even when the view count is well over the line.

AdSense: the step that stops more applications than creators expect

Meeting the numbers is not enough on its own. You also have to complete the AdSense step in YouTube Studio. You may have only one AdSense account per payee, so a duplicate account — even an old, forgotten one — can block approval. Create your AdSense account only in YouTube Studio. If you need to change the linked account, you can do that only once per 32 days. Check that the name on your AdSense account matches your official identification, that your address is one where you can actually receive post, and that any verification is complete. Never share your Google password with anyone offering to fix AdSense; no legitimate helper needs it.

Step-by-step: plan a clean application before 1 February 2027

  1. Check your real numbers today in YouTube Studio: subscribers, valid public watch hours for 12 months, and valid public Shorts views for 90 days.
  2. Pick one route, long-form or Shorts, whichever you are genuinely closer to, and plan around that one.
  3. Work out your pace from your last 28 days, allowing for older viewing that will fall out of the window.
  4. Review your whole channel for reused content. Flag compilations and re-uploads. Reused content applies channel-wide, even with permission, because permission answers the copyright question, not whether the content is meaningfully yours.
  5. Review your format for inauthentic content. If your videos follow the same template with minimal variation, a reviewer may see them as repetitious or mass-produced. Inauthentic content was renamed on 15 July 2025.
  6. Sort AdSense early: one account, created in YouTube Studio, with your real name and address, and verification complete.
  7. Apply only when all three are ready: numbers met, content reviewed, AdSense ready.

Checklist: are you ready to apply?

  • 1,000 real subscribers, not bought ones
  • 4,000 valid public watch hours in 12 months, or 10 million valid public Shorts views in 90 days, checked today
  • Your pace is steady, not a single old spike about to fall out of the window
  • Every public video passes the test: is this meaningfully mine?
  • No compilation or re-upload risks hiding in older videos
  • Your format is not a repeated template with minimal variation
  • One AdSense account only, created in YouTube Studio, with your real name and address
  • No active strikes — a Content ID claim is not a strike, three strikes in 90 days can lead to termination, and strikes expire after 90 days
  • You can explain on camera, if asked, how you make your videos
  • You have written down your dates: application day, the 21-day appeal deadline, and the 30-day reapply date

What happens if you are rejected?

A rejection is not the end of your channel, but it starts a clock. You have 21 days to appeal. If you do not appeal, or your appeal fails, you can reapply 30 days after a first rejection, and 90 days after later rejections or a failed appeal. A rejection in January, followed by a 30-day wait, lands after the thresholds have doubled. Read the exact policy it names first: reused content and inauthentic content need different fixes. Frequently asked questions

What are the YouTube monetization requirements in 2027?

From 1 February 2027, new applicants need 1,000 subscribers plus either 8,000 valid public watch hours in 12 months or 20 million valid public Shorts views in 90 days. Before that date, the bar is 4,000 hours or 10 million Shorts views.

I am already monetized. Do I need 8,000 hours from 1 February 2027?

No. The change applies to new applicants. Channels already in the YouTube Partner Program are not required to re-qualify at the new, higher level simply because the date passed.

Do I need both 8,000 hours and 20 million Shorts views?

No. You need one route, plus 1,000 subscribers. Either the long-form route or the Shorts route is enough, as long as you pass the content and AdSense checks.

What happens if I am rejected just before 1 February 2027?

You have 21 days to appeal. If you do not appeal, or your appeal fails, you can reapply 30 days after a first rejection. That date would fall after the thresholds rise, so you would then need to meet the new, higher bar.

Can I buy subscribers or watch time to reach the thresholds faster?

No. Bought subscribers, fake views, and purchased watch time break YouTube's policies and can get a channel terminated. They also do not help you pass the content review.

What to do next

Start with your numbers in YouTube Studio today: subscribers, 12-month watch hours, and 90-day Shorts views, with the date. Pick your route and work out your pace. If you are close to the current thresholds and your content is clean, plan a careful application before 1 February 2027.
