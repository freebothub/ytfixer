Know your dates

Rejected? Your clock is already running

The most expensive mistake is doing nothing while the windows close. Here is the timeline every rejected creator is on.

Day 0

Rejection email arrives

Read the exact policy named. “Reused content” and “inauthentic content” need different fixes — don’t guess.

21 days

Appeal window

You have 21 days to appeal. A good appeal is a short video showing your face, your process, and what changed — not a complaint.

30 days

First reapply date

If the appeal fails (or you skip it), you can reapply after 30 days — but only reapply after you have actually fixed the flagged content.

90 days

Later reapplications

After a second rejection the wait becomes 90 days. This is why the first fix has to be the right fix.

📅
Coming 1 February 2027: the bar doubles

For new applicants, YPP thresholds rise to 8,000 watch hours (from 4,000) or 20M Shorts views (from 10M). Existing partners are not affected. If you are close to today’s thresholds, applying cleanly before the change matters — read the guide in the blog below.

FAQ

Questions every rejected creator asks

Can you guarantee I’ll get monetized?

No — and run from anyone who says yes. Only YouTube decides. What we guarantee is an honest, policy-based diagnosis: the real reason, the exact fixes, and a properly built appeal. That is the part actually in your control.

My content is from other creators, but I have permission. Why was I rejected?

Because “reused content” is a separate policy from copyright. Permission solves the copyright question — it does not make the content yours. YouTube wants significant original commentary, transformation or educational value added by you. Our reused content guide explains this in full.

Should I appeal or just reapply after 30 days?

Appeal if you genuinely fixed the problem and can show your creation process on camera within 21 days. Reapply if the channel still needs real changes — a rushed appeal that fails leaves you on the 30-day (then 90-day) clock anyway. This is the single most valuable decision we help with.

I’m stuck on AdSense Step 2. Can you log in and fix it?

We never ask for or use your passwords. We diagnose where you’re stuck — identity, address, PIN, duplicate account — and guide you step by step to clear it yourself, safely.

Do you sell channels, subscribers or watch time?

No. No channel sales, no subscribers, no views, no watch time. Those violate YouTube policy, put buyers’ channels at risk, and AdSense can never be transferred. We only do audits, guidance and honest growth diagnosis.

What changes on 1 February 2027?

For new YPP applicants, thresholds double: 8,000 watch hours (from 4,000) or 20M Shorts views in 90 days (from 10M), alongside 1,000 subscribers. Channels already in YPP are not affected. If you’re near today’s line, a clean application before the change is worth planning for.
