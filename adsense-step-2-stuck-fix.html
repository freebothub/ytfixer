AdSense Step 2 Stuck? How to Fix YouTube AdSense Linking

Guide · Updated 8 November 2026 · by the YTFixer Team

You applied to the YouTube Partner Program, passed the first checks, and then everything stopped. No approval, no clear rejection, just AdSense Step 2 sitting unfinished. Step 2 is where many applications quietly stall, and it is one of the most fixable problems a creator can face, once you know where you are stuck.

This guide explains what Step 2 is, why it gets stuck, and how to work through it in order. It uses only the rules YouTube actually applies. Only YouTube decides whether your channel is accepted. What you can control is getting the AdSense side clean, complete, and honest. Read the whole guide before you change anything, because fixing the wrong thing first can create a second problem.

What AdSense Step 2 actually is

When you apply to the Partner Program, one step is linking an AdSense account. AdSense handles payments to creators, so YouTube needs a working account linked to your channel before it can pay you. Step 2 is not a test of your videos. It is a check of your payment identity: who you are, where you live, and whether one clear, verified account can receive money for this channel.

That is why a channel with good videos can still sit stuck at Step 2. The videos may be fine, but the payment setup may not be. Studio may show the step as pending or incomplete without saying which detail is missing. The cause is usually one of a small number of common problems, which this guide works through one by one.

Why AdSense Step 2 gets stuck

Most cases come down to identity, address, or duplication. Your name may not match your official identity document exactly. Your address may not be one where post can reach you. You may have an old AdSense account you forgot about. Or part of the payments profile may be half finished.

Another cause is starting in the wrong place. AdSense for YouTube should be created only in YouTube Studio, through the application itself. Changing things too often while waiting can also hurt: you can change the linked AdSense account only once per 32 days, so a hasty change can lock you into a wait you did not plan for. Find the stuck point first, then change one thing at a time.

The step-by-step fix

Work through these steps in order. Stop at the step that matches your problem, fix that one thing, and check your status again before moving on.

  1. Use YouTube Studio only. Create or link AdSense from inside YouTube Studio, through the Partner Program application. Do not create a separate account elsewhere first.
  2. Check for a second AdSense account. YouTube allows one AdSense account per payee. If you already have one, even an old one, that is the account to use. A duplicate is a common hidden cause of a stuck Step 2, so search your email accounts, including old addresses, before creating anything new.
  3. Match your name to your identity document exactly. Spelling, order, and any middle names must match. A nickname or a name in a different order can cause a mismatch.
  4. Use an address where post can reach you. Verification mail is sent to that address, so it must be real and complete. Use the address on your official documents where possible and check every line for typing errors.
  5. Submit clear identity verification. Make sure the document image is clear and readable, with the whole document visible. A blurry photo or a cropped corner can be rejected without a clear reason shown.
  6. Complete your payments profile. Tax and payment details left half done can keep the step pending. Fill in every required field, and use the correct option where a field does not apply, rather than leaving it blank.
  7. Check your country. Your AdSense country should be the country where you genuinely live and can verify your identity and address. A mismatch between country, documents, and address is a common reason for a step to stay stuck.
  8. Wait, then check again. Some checks take time. Once you have fixed the real problem, give the system time to update. Do not keep changing the linked account or resubmitting the same document while you wait.

The duplicate account trap

Many people have an AdSense account they do not remember, made for a blog, a website, or an old channel, perhaps with a different email address. To them, it does not exist. To YouTube, it does, and the one-account-per-payee rule means a new account cannot simply replace it. If you find an old account, link that one.

If you cannot access the old account at all, do not create a duplicate and do not ask anyone else to create one for you. Use the official help and recovery options in your Google account, or follow the guidance YouTube gives in Studio and in YouTube Help. That is slower than starting again, but starting again is what keeps the step stuck.

What never to do while fixing Step 2

Never share your Google password with anyone who offers to fix Step 2. No legitimate helper needs it, not us, not anyone on a freelance site, not a stranger on social media. Anyone asking for it is a risk to your channel and your money. Real help is guidance: someone tells you where you are stuck, and you clear it yourself.

Never pay someone who promises to approve your AdSense account or your application for a fee. Approval is YouTube’s decision alone. Never create an AdSense account in someone else’s name, either: the account must belong to the payee, the person who will actually be paid. And do not buy subscribers, views, or watch time. That breaks YouTube’s policies and can get a channel terminated. Thresholds are 1,000 subscribers and 4,000 valid public watch hours in 12 months, or 10 million valid public Shorts views in 90 days, and they must be met honestly. From 1 February 2027, new applicants will need 8,000 watch hours or 20 million Shorts views.

Checklist: AdSense Step 2 ready

Use this checklist before you check your status again. Every box should be honestly ticked.

  • AdSense was created or linked only in YouTube Studio
  • One AdSense account for you as payee, with no duplicate or forgotten old account
  • Name on AdSense matches your official identity document exactly
  • Address is complete, current, and able to receive post
  • Identity verification submitted with a clear, readable document
  • Payments profile complete, including tax and payment details
  • AdSense country is the country where you genuinely live
  • Linked account not changed in the last 32 days without a clear reason
  • No one else has your Google password, and no one was paid to “approve” your account

Frequently asked questions

How long does AdSense Step 2 take?

There is no fixed time for every channel. Some steps clear quickly once the missing detail is fixed, and others take longer while verification is checked. Fix the real problem, submit clear documents, and check Studio again after giving the system time to update. Changing things repeatedly while you wait usually slows things down.

Can I change the AdSense account linked to my channel?

Yes, but only once per 32 days. Before you change it, be sure the current account is genuinely the wrong one, for example because it belongs to someone else or cannot be verified. If the current account is yours and can be fixed, fix it instead of switching.

I already have an AdSense account. Should I create a new one for YouTube?

No. YouTube allows one AdSense account per payee, so link the account you already have. Creating a second account is one of the most common reasons Step 2 gets stuck.

Someone offered to fix my Step 2 if I share my password. Is that safe?

No. Never share your Google password with anyone. No legitimate helper needs it, and sharing it puts your channel, your email, and your future payments at risk.

What to do next

Start with the status in YouTube Studio and the checklist above. Fix the one problem that matches your status, submit clear documents, and complete the payments profile. Then give it time and check again. If the step is still stuck, get a second pair of eyes on the exact wording Studio is showing you, rather than starting again from scratch.

AdSense is only one part of the application. Your channel still has to meet the thresholds and pass the content review. If you have a rejection, remember you have 21 days to appeal, you can reapply 30 days after a first rejection, and 90 days after later rejections or a failed appeal. Read our guide on appeal vs reapply and the pre-application checklist, and read the 2027 requirements before that change. No guide, no audit, and no payment can guarantee YouTube will accept your channel. A careful fix removes the AdSense obstacle, so your channel is judged on its content, which you can keep improving, video by video.
