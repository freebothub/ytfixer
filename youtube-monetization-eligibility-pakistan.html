YouTube Monetization in Pakistan: Eligibility, AdSense and What to Check in Studio

Guide · Updated November 2026 · About 8 min read · by the YTFixer Team

If you are a creator in Pakistan, you have probably heard two very different stories about YouTube monetization. One says it is easy: reach the numbers, apply, and start earning. The other says it is impossible from Pakistan, or that you need a trick, a foreign account, or a shortcut to get approved. Neither story is the full truth. Monetization from Pakistan follows the same YouTube Partner Program rules as anywhere else, and the only reliable place to check eligibility is YouTube Studio itself. This guide explains the current thresholds, the change coming on 1 February 2027, how AdSense works, and the checks to run in Studio before you apply. It does not invent Pakistan-specific thresholds, payment methods, or approval rates.

The current YouTube Partner Program thresholds

To apply for the YouTube Partner Program today, a channel normally needs 1,000 subscribers and either 4,000 hours of valid public watch time in the last 12 months, or 10 million valid public Shorts views in the last 90 days. Those are the headline numbers, but they are not the whole test. YouTube also checks that your channel follows its monetization policies, has no active Community Guidelines strikes, has two-step verification on, and has an AdSense account linked correctly. A channel can pass the numbers and still be rejected. Only valid public watch time counts. Buying subscribers, views, or watch time breaks YouTube’s policies and can end a channel.

The change coming on 1 February 2027

From 1 February 2027, new applicants will need 8,000 hours of valid public watch time, or 20 million valid public Shorts views, instead of the current 4,000 hours or 10 million Shorts views. The subscriber requirement remains 1,000. A rejected application costs you time: you can reapply 30 days after a first rejection, and 90 days after later rejections or a failed appeal. This guide does not promise that an expanded tier is available in Pakistan. As the expanded-tier country list appeared on 8 November 2026, Pakistan did not appear in it. Check eligibility in YouTube Studio for your own channel, on the day you apply.

How to check your eligibility in YouTube Studio

YouTube Studio is the only reliable source for your own eligibility, and checking it takes only a few minutes. Do not trust a screenshot from another creator, and do not trust a third-party website that claims to check eligibility for you.

  1. Open YouTube Studio and go to Earn. Sign in with the Google account that owns your channel.
  2. Read the progress for subscribers and watch time. Check whether the figures are for the last 12 months for long-form watch time, or the last 90 days for Shorts views.
  3. Look for any policy or eligibility warning. Read each warning in full. Do not skip a step because it looks small.
  4. Check that two-step verification is on. YouTube requires it on the Google account for your channel before you can apply.
  5. Check your AdSense step in Studio. Create and link it only through the steps shown in Studio.
  6. Note what is still missing. Write down the exact gap: subscribers, hours, or an incomplete step.

AdSense setup: one account, in Studio, in your real name

AdSense is how YouTube pays creators, and the setup rules are strict. Use only your real details.

  • One AdSense account per payee. Each person or business that is paid should have one AdSense account.
  • Create it only in YouTube Studio. Follow the link YouTube gives you in the Earn section.
  • Use your real name and address. Enter them exactly as they appear on your official documents. A mismatch can delay or block payments.
  • You can change the linked AdSense account only once per 32 days. A rushed change can lock you into the wrong account for a month. Check the details carefully before you link an account.
  • Never share your password. No one who offers to help you with AdSense or monetization needs your Google or AdSense password. Anyone who asks for it is not offering genuine help.
  • Do not invent a payment method for Pakistan. This guide lists none, because options can change. Check the payment options shown in your own AdSense account.

Rejections creators in Pakistan see most often

Creators in Pakistan are rejected for the same policy reasons as creators anywhere else. There is no special Pakistan rejection, and there is no Pakistan shortcut. Reused content is the most common. It means YouTube did not see enough original value in the channel. Compilations, re-uploads from TikTok or television, slideshows read aloud over stock footage, and reaction videos with almost no reaction all fall into this group. Permission from the original creator does not fix it. The fix is to add significant original commentary, your own footage, or your own research. Inauthentic content is about repetitious, mass-produced, or templated videos that look as if they were made to game the system rather than to help a viewer. AdSense problems are the third group. An application can stall if the AdSense account is not set up correctly, if the details do not match, or if there is an invalid traffic issue on the account. Copyright and Community Guidelines issues also block applications. Fix strikes and problem videos before you apply.

Before you apply: the channel check

Before you press apply, look at your channel as a reviewer would. Check each public video: is it clearly yours, does it add something original, and does the title and thumbnail honestly match what is inside? Remove or set to private any pure re-upload, compilation with almost no added value, or video that breaks a policy. Publish new videos where your face, your voice, or your original writing is obvious from the first ten seconds. A reviewer looks at the whole channel, not only at your best video, so fix any problem properly before you apply.

Step-by-step: apply the right way

  1. Confirm the numbers in Studio. Check that you meet 1,000 subscribers and either 4,000 hours in the last 12 months or 10 million Shorts views in the last 90 days.
  2. Finish every Studio step. Turn on two-step verification, clear any policy warning, and complete the AdSense step only through YouTube Studio, with your real name and address.
  3. Audit your public videos. Remove or remake anything that is reused, inauthentic, or against the policies, and publish new, clearly original videos first.
  4. Read the appeal rules before you apply. You have 21 days to appeal a rejection. You can reapply 30 days after a first rejection, and 90 days after later rejections or a failed appeal.
  5. Apply from Studio and wait. Submit the application from the Earn section and wait for YouTube’s review. Do not pay anyone who promises to get you approved.
  6. If you are rejected, read the exact reason. Do not guess. The reason in the email tells you which fix is needed, and the fix for one reason will not help with another.

Checklist: are you ready to apply?

  • You have 1,000 subscribers and either 4,000 hours in the last 12 months or 10 million Shorts views in the last 90 days.
  • You know that from 1 February 2027 new applicants need 8,000 hours or 20 million Shorts views.
  • You checked eligibility in YouTube Studio for your own channel, and you are not relying on another country’s tier.
  • Two-step verification is on, with no active strikes or unresolved policy warnings in Studio.
  • Your AdSense account was created only in YouTube Studio, with one account per payee.
  • Every public video is meaningfully yours, with no pure re-uploads hiding in older uploads.
  • You have not bought subscribers, views, or watch time, and you have not used a service that promises fast growth.
  • You know your appeal deadline is 21 days, and your reapply dates are 30 days after a first rejection and 90 days after later rejections.
  • You have not shared your Google or AdSense password with anyone, and you have not paid anyone who promised approval.

Frequently asked questions

Is YouTube monetization available in Pakistan?

Check eligibility in YouTube Studio for your own channel. This guide does not promise an expanded tier is available in Pakistan, because Pakistan did not appear in the expanded-tier country list as rendered on 8 November 2026.

What are the current thresholds, and what changes in February 2027?

Today you normally need 1,000 subscribers and either 4,000 hours in the last 12 months or 10 million Shorts views in the last 90 days. From 1 February 2027, new applicants need 8,000 hours or 20 million Shorts views.

Can I use a relative’s AdSense account to apply faster?

No. AdSense allows one account per payee, and the account should be in the name of the person who will be paid, with matching real details. Create and link your AdSense account only in YouTube Studio.

How long do I have to appeal or reapply if I am rejected?

You have 21 days to appeal a YPP rejection. You can reapply 30 days after a first rejection, and 90 days after later rejections or a failed appeal.

Will buying watch time help me reach 4,000 hours?

No. Bought subscribers, views, or watch time break YouTube’s policies and can end a channel.
